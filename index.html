01 / INTRODUCTION

About.

I study how public institutions meet a changing world.

I'm a student of public administration who enjoys asking how institutions work and how they can work better. My interests sit at the intersection of public policy, digital governance, and everyday social life. I read widely, follow new technologies with curiosity, and try to connect ideas from research with problems people actually face. Through coursework, fieldwork, writing, and small coding experiments, I am learning to turn careful observation into useful questions and clear communication. This site is a place to collect that work, reflect on what I learn, and share projects as they grow.
